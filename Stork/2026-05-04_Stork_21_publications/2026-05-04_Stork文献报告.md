## Paper 1: Freestanding Flexible Vortex Tubes Integrated With Ferroelectric Transistor

### 基本信息
- **标题**: Freestanding Flexible Vortex Tubes Integrated With Ferroelectric Transistor
- **作者**: Feng-Hui Gong, Lei Tang, Yu-Ting Chen, et al.
- **期刊**: Advanced Materials (IF: 26.8)
- **DOI**: 10.1002/adma.202522292
- **PMID**: 42068198
- **下载状态**: ✅ 已下载
- **PDF**: Gong_2026_Vortex_Tubes_FeFET.pdf

### 摘要翻译
**英文原文**: Polar topologies hold great potential in information storage. So far, thin films with polar topological structure are predominantly constrained by the substrate on which they are grown. Such substrate-stabilized polar topologies limit their integration into memory devices, and consequently, their physical behaviors in data memory remain unclear. Here, we show a ferroelectric field-effect transistor (Fe-FET) memory device, which is based on large-scale freestanding pure polar vortex tube arrays. In situ heating demonstrates that vortex tube arrays in freestanding superlattices undergo a more directly reversible phase transition, evolving from vortex tubes to single domains and ultimately to the disappearance of ferroelectricity. The flexible vortex tubes, which are obtained by removing the sacrificial layers, could bend at a 90° without breaking, demonstrating the nature of robustness. The Fe-FET exhibits a wide and stable clockwise hysteresis loop, spanning a voltage range of -60 to 60 V and temperatures from room temperature to 450 K. Due to the stable and robust ferroelectric vortex dipole moment arrays, the Fe-FET achieves a memory retention time of 3600 s and an endurance of 10⁴ cycles. This work provides a platform for the integration of polar topological structures into flexible memory devices, clarifying their prospects in next-generation data storage.

**中文翻译**: 极性拓扑结构在信息存储中具有巨大潜力。迄今为止，具有极性拓扑结构的薄膜主要受限于其生长所用的衬底。这种衬底稳定的极性拓扑限制了它们在存储器件中的集成，因此它们在数据存储中的物理行为仍不清楚。本文展示了一种基于大规模自支撑纯极性涡旋管阵列的铁电场效应晶体管（Fe-FET）存储器件。原位加热表明，自支撑超晶格中的涡旋管阵列经历了一个更直接可逆的相变，从涡旋管演变为单畴，最终铁电性消失。通过去除牺牲层获得的柔性涡旋管可以在 90° 弯曲而不断裂，展示了其鲁棒性。该 Fe-FET 展现了宽且稳定的顺时针回滞环，覆盖 -60 至 60 V 的电压范围和室温至 450 K 的温度范围。由于稳定且鲁棒的铁电涡旋偶极矩阵列，该 Fe-FET 实现了 3600 秒的存储保持时间和 10⁴ 次的耐久性。这项工作为将极性拓扑结构集成到柔性存储器件中提供了平台，阐明了它们在下一代数据存储中的前景。

## Paper 2: Sliding Ferroelectricity in MoS₂/ReS₂ Heterojunction for a Sensitive and Reconfigurable Bioelectronic Interface

### 基本信息
- **标题**: Sliding Ferroelectricity in MoS₂/ReS₂ Heterojunction for a Sensitive and Reconfigurable Bioelectronic Interface
- **作者**: Weijia Mu, Changming Ke, Ning Guo, et al.
- **期刊**: ACS Nano (IF: 16)
- **DOI**: 10.1021/acsnano.6c01271
- **PMID**: 42055543
- **下载状态**: ✅ 已下载

### 摘要翻译
**英文原文**: Two-dimensional (2D) sliding ferroelectrics promise an ultrathin, fatigue-free platform for reconfigurable electronics, but their development has been hindered by a strict requirement for lattice matching, which limits the material choice and functionality. Here, we overcome this limitation by demonstrating room-temperature sliding ferroelectricity in a lattice-mismatched semiconducting MoS₂/ReS₂ heterobilayer (1.40% mismatch along a-axis, 2.32% along b-axis), greatly expanding the material scope for sliding ferroelectrics. The sliding ferroelectricity is revealed by first-principles calculations, second harmonic generation, piezoresponse force microscopy, and conductive atomic force microscopy. Notably, the sliding ferroelectricity endows the heterobilayer with submicrosecond switching dynamics (<1 μs pulses), exceptional endurance exceeding 10⁵ cycles without wake-up effect, and a tunneling electroresistance ratio up to 10.4. Furthermore, we construct a ferroelectric field-effect transistor-based biosensor (bio-FeFET) for label-free detection of 8-hydroxy-2′-deoxyguanosine (8-OHdG), a biomarker for oxidative DNA damage. The bio-FeFET achieves a sensitivity of 12.57% per decade, a limit of detection down to 5.6 × 10⁻¹³ M (560 fM), and reconfigurable "write-read-erase" operation enabling electrical regeneration of the sensing interface. This work unveils sliding ferroelectricity in lattice-mismatched 2D heterostructures and paves the way for their applications in reconfigurable bioelectronics.

**中文翻译**: 二维滑移铁电体为实现超薄、无疲劳的可重构电子学提供了有前景的平台，但其发展受到严格晶格匹配要求的阻碍，限制了材料选择和功能性。本文在晶格失配的 MoS₂/ReS₂ 半导体异质双层（a 轴失配 1.40%，b 轴失配 2.32%）中演示了室温滑移铁电性，极大地扩展了滑移铁电体的材料范围。通过第一性原理计算、二次谐波产生、压电力显微镜和导电原子力显微镜揭示了滑移铁电性。值得注意的是，滑移铁电性赋予异质双层亚微秒级开关动力学（<1 μs 脉冲）、超过 10⁵ 次的优异耐久性（无唤醒效应）以及高达 10.4 的隧穿电阻比。此外，我们构建了基于铁电场效应晶体管的生物传感器（bio-FeFET）用于无标记检测 8-羟基-2′-脱氧鸟苷（8-OHdG），一种氧化性 DNA 损伤的生物标志物。该 bio-FeFET 实现了每 decade 12.57% 的灵敏度，低至 5.6 × 10⁻¹³ M (560 fM) 的检测限，以及可重构的"写入-读取-擦除"操作，实现传感界面的电再生。这项工作揭示了晶格失配二维异质结构中的滑移铁电性，为其在可重构生物电子学中的应用铺平了道路。
