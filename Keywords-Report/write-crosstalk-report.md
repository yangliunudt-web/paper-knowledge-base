### Key Observations

- **Write crosstalk (写入串扰)** is most commonly referred to as **"write disturb"** or **"写入干扰"** in the FeFET literature. The Chinese term "写入串扰" is less commonly used in the academic papers themselves; instead "写入干扰" (write disturb) is the standard Chinese translation.
- The primary mitigation strategies fall into three categories: (1) **inhibition bias schemes** (VW/2, VW/3), (2) **cell structure modifications** (adding selection transistors such as 1T-1FeFET, 2T2C, 1T1T), and (3) **write scheme optimization** (LCSS, self-compensated writing).
- There is a **clear progression** from early work (2016-2018) identifying the problem, to intermediate work (2021-2023) characterizing it in different array architectures, to recent work (2024-2025) providing integrated solutions.
- Most published work focuses on **FeFET-based arrays**; **ReRAM/memristor crossbars** face analogous sneak-path crosstalk challenges but with different physical mechanisms.
